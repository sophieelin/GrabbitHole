## Project Summary

**Bad situations:**
- Group members easily lose track of how their research findings connect
- Researchers forget interesting leads they wanted to revisit
- Difficult to find and build on previously completed research paths

**Solution:** Grabbithole lets researchers save & visualize research discovery paths as graphs of connected links. Users can collaborate on shared graphs, fork others' research, propose contributions, and search public research graphs.
